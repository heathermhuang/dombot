# Design Context

## Users and purpose

Domain investors managing holdings across multiple registrar accounts, including new domainers visiting the free SaaS offer from a conference promotion. They need to understand inventory, expirations, renewal estimates, selective publication and AI-assistant access.

## Brand direction

The owner rejected the d.d logo and the previous oversized-copy landing page. Use the full domains.domains wordmark. Working visual direction for this correction: precise, composed, practical; an orderly portfolio ledger rather than a generic promotional page. Retain the restrained forest/neutral palette, with clearer product evidence and more useful hierarchy.

## Design principles

- Show what the product does in the first screen, with a clearly labelled example portfolio.
- Use a readable full-name wordmark; no d.d monogram or alternate lettermark.
- Keep marketing secondary to clear actions: sign up, sign in, explore features and visit the public collection.
- Use owner-approved generic domains from the publicly listed portfolio in marketing previews, rather than .example names or brand-like holdings. Use branded SaaS captures; label illustrative registrar details, renewal estimates and draft statuses. Do not invent asking prices or imply live publication. Retain upstream credit in the shared footer, source offer and legal notices.
- Keep mobile content complete and readable, and respect existing authentication and release constraints.

## Product language and attribution

- Lead with the product and actions: visible product screenshots, an immediate demo link and a clear signup button. Keep benefits and setup steps short; the owner rejected the long explanatory landing page. Explain registrar connection and CSV import in concise steps. Account refers to a sign-in; qualify “registrar account” when discussing a provider login. Do not describe the portfolio as an account.
- Use the exact owner-approved attribution: “domains.domains is a hosted, multi-tenant version of aoxborrow/dombot.” Link that repository identity upstream, separately from the open-source SaaS fork. Keep the original author credit in source/legal notices without attaching it to domains.domains. Use domains.domains as the public brand. Mention Magic Crystal Limited only where needed to identify the legal operator in Terms and Privacy; keep that operator distinct from the upstream author.

## Managed SaaS presentation

- Render registrar marks in one ink color, retaining their shapes and adjacent names.
- Keep Archivo and the forest/neutral palette. Use warm off-white page backgrounds, white working surfaces, forest primary actions, and restrained elevation. Give the dark theme the same hierarchy with deep green surfaces.
- Hosted navigation uses compact, fully rounded rectangular active states rather than browser-style tabs. Scope this treatment to gateway authentication; retain the upstream desktop and self-hosted presentation.
- Account forms use a bounded, readable white surface with generous field spacing and clear focus states. Preserve all legal, error, recovery, and authentication controls.
- Use surfaces to distinguish a working table or form from the surrounding page. Keep sample-data disclosures adjacent to product illustrations, preserve mobile details, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

- New managed sessions default to light mode; saved theme preferences take precedence. Apply the managed palette to body-mounted dialogs and menus as well as the app shell.

- Customer-facing copy leads with domains.domains and product tasks. Do not repeat DomBot/upstream provenance in hero text, screenshot captions, demo buttons or onboarding instructions; the shared footer, source page and legal notices carry that attribution. Keep Sample data labels.
