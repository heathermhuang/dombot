# Design Context

## Users and purpose

Domain investors managing holdings across multiple registrar accounts, including new domainers visiting the free SaaS offer from a conference promotion. They need to understand inventory, expirations, renewal estimates, selective publication and AI-assistant access.

## Brand direction

The owner rejected the d.d logo and the previous oversized-copy landing page. Use the full domains.domains wordmark. Working visual direction for this correction: precise, composed, practical; an orderly portfolio ledger rather than a generic promotional page. Retain the restrained forest/neutral palette, with clearer product evidence and more useful hierarchy.

## Design principles

- Show what the product does in the first screen, with a clearly labelled example portfolio.
- Use a readable full-name wordmark; no d.d monogram or alternate lettermark.
- Keep marketing secondary to clear actions: sign up, sign in, explore features and visit the public collection.
- Use reserved .example names for invented illustrations. Unmodified upstream product screenshots may show their public demo data when visibly credited as original DomBot sample data. Never present them as hosted customer holdings or actual quotes.
- Keep mobile content complete and readable, and respect existing authentication and release constraints.

## Product language and attribution

- Lead with the product and actions: visible DomBot screenshots, an immediate demo link and a clear signup button. Keep benefits and setup steps short; the owner rejected the long explanatory landing page. Explain registrar connection and CSV import in concise steps. Account refers to a sign-in; qualify “registrar account” when discussing a provider login. Do not describe the portfolio as an account.
- Use the exact owner-approved attribution: “domains.domains is a hosted, multi-tenant version of aoxborrow/dombot.” Link that repository identity upstream, separately from the open-source SaaS fork. Keep the original author credit in source/legal notices without attaching it to domains.domains. Use domains.domains as the public brand. Mention Magic Crystal Limited only where needed to identify the legal operator in Terms and Privacy; keep that operator distinct from the upstream author.
